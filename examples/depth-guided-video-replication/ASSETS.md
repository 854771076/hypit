# Selected production assets

Replace every fixture below with a reviewed production asset before a paid Build. A changed identity,
costume, prop state or location state is a new version.

## character:host / appearance:v1

- canonical facts: approved face, hairstyle, body proportions, grooming and makeup
- costume signature: approved clothing, footwear and accessories
- performance anchors: upright posture, stable gait and destination-directed gaze
- selected board: `assets/character.png` (fixture only)
- review: blocked until the original-resolution character board passes identity and appearance review
- forbidden drift: alternate face, age class, hairstyle, costume or body type

## scene:primary / version:v1

- geometry: approved entrances, exits, camera side and foreground/midground/background anchors
- light and look: motivated practical light, natural skin texture, readable background, restrained
  diffusion, no glossy commercial grade and no shallow cinematic bokeh
- selected board: `assets/character.png` (fixture only)
- review: blocked until replaced by an original-resolution scene board

## prop:primary / state:v1

- form and state: approved scale, material, color, markings, ownership and persistent state
- selected board: `assets/character.png` (fixture only)
- review: blocked until replaced by an original-resolution prop board

## storyboard:S01 / temporal:v1

- purpose: approved composition, action stages and reading order for one atomic shot
- selected board: `assets/character.png` (fixture only)
- review: blocked until replaced and approved; the final video must never reproduce its grid or labels

## source:reference-shot / analysis-only:v1

- selected file: `assets/original-shot.mp4`
- allowed use: analysis and RunningHub depth preprocessing only
- forbidden use: final picture, final audio, Timeline patch, Caption source or generated-shot audio
